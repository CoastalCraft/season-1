The first chapter

It started with Coastal Adventures.

CoastalCraft’s story began as Coastal Adventures. It was started by Hybrinit, who sought out members through the old Minecraft forums.

Before Sakura Shores and The Mighty Mesa Reborn, this was our first chapter: the beginning of the story we now call CoastalCraft.

Unfortunately, all backups of that original world have been lost. But the memories it created and the legacy it built will never be lost.
